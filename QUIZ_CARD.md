# Purple, Green, or Orange?

Set the switches to your answer (A, B or C) and press the button. The display shows the question number; after question 15 it shows your colour.

**1. Your ideal Saturday morning:**

- **A** Cold-calling leads before the competition wakes up.
- **B** Building an LED badge that plays a tune when you shake it.
- **C** Laundry. Maybe. I haven't decided.

**2. Someone sends you a calendar invite titled 'quick sync'. You:**

- **A** Bring a whiteboard marker and a diagram nobody asked for.
- **B** Accept it, then forget which meeting it was.
- **C** Turn it into an upsell before the agenda loads.

**3. Your phone's home screen is:**

- **A** The default wallpaper. Still.
- **B** A CRM with 47 notifications, every one an opportunity.
- **C** A terminal app, a synth app and a folder of memes.

**4. A colleague's change breaks the build. You:**

- **A** Bisect it, fix it, and send them a gif of the culprit commit.
- **B** Sell them a premium support plan.
- **C** Wait for someone else to notice.

**5. Pick a superpower:**

- **A** Closing any deal with a single handshake.
- **B** Sorry, what were the options again?
- **C** Seeing radio waves.

**6. Your LinkedIn headline:**

- **A** Breaks things professionally, fixes them for fun.
- **B** Employee.
- **C** Revenue Ninja | Closer | Hustle 24/7 | DM me

**7. At a party, you're most likely:**

- **A** Standing near the snacks, nodding.
- **B** Handing your business card to the host's grandma.
- **C** Explaining why the speakers hum, then fixing it.

**8. Favourite number:**

- **A** 0x2A.
- **B** Quota, plus 30 percent.
- **C** Seven? Everyone says seven.

**9. A new gadget arrives. You:**

- **A** Resell it at a markup before opening it.
- **B** Open it with a screwdriver before reading the manual.
- **C** Leave it in the box for a month.

**10. Your email sign-off:**

- **A** Regards.
- **B** Cheers, sent from my hand-wired keyboard.
- **C** Let's circle back at 7 am tomorrow. Sent from my car.

**11. Someone tells you 'no'. You:**

- **A** Hear 'not yet' and follow up in five minutes.
- **B** Accept it. It was probably the right answer anyway.
- **C** Ask for the error message and start debugging.

**12. Pick a lunch:**

- **A** Whatever the vending machine gives after the Konami code.
- **B** Plain toast.
- **C** Steak with a client, expensed.

**13. Your desk has:**

- **A** A stapler. Nothing else.
- **B** A gong you hit every time you close a deal.
- **C** An oscilloscope, a rubber duck and three half-built projects.

**14. Your dream job title:**

- **A** Chief Revenue Officer, by 30.
- **B** Chip designer who also makes the demo video.
- **C** Whatever pays, I suppose.

**15. This quiz is running on a chip you could hold on a fingertip. Your reaction:**

- **A** Okay.
- **B** Can I see the layout?
- **C** How many units can I pre-sell?

**Results:** Purple: Fun and technical. You read datasheets for pleasure and still get invited to parties. · Green: Pure sales energy. You'd sell this chip back to the people who made it, at a markup. · Orange: Neither. A little lost, a little beige, and honestly that's fine.
